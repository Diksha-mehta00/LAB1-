🔎 Activity 3 — Retrieved Information vs Generated Information
A major concept explored in this lab was the difference between retrieved information and AI-generated information.

Retrieved Information
Information obtained from a specific and existing source, such as:

Airline official website
Airport official website
Government website
Official aviation document
Generated Information
New content produced by an AI model based on patterns and information learned by the model.

Why Verification Matters
AI-generated information may be:

Outdated
Incomplete
Incorrect
Missing recent changes
This is particularly important in aviation because flight status, baggage rules, regulations, passenger rights, safety procedures, and operational information can change.

Key Principle
AI can help find, explain and summarize information, but important aviation information should be verified using current and official sources.

🧠 Activity 4 — AI Capabilities in Aviation
The lab identified four important AI capabilities.

AI Capability	Meaning	Aviation Example
Prediction	Estimates what may happen in the future	Predicting flight delays
Classification	Assigns information to a category	Classifying passenger complaints
Recommendation	Suggests a suitable option	Recommending a passenger seat
Generation	Creates new content	Generating a flight-delay announcement
Business Problem First
An aviation manager should first understand the business problem and then select the appropriate AI capability.

For example:

Flight delay → Prediction
Complaint categorisation → Classification
Seat selection → Recommendation
Passenger communication → Generation
